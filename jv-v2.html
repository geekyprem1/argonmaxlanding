How do I get my affiliate link?+
Use the “Get your affiliate link” button to open Launchpad JV. Sign in or create your account, then find the ArgonMAX AI offer when its affiliate listing is available. Replace the placeholder in your email swipes with your approved tracking link.
Is every tool included in the front end?+
The page introduces 13 planned platform modules. Specific allowances, premium features, team seats, agency, branding and reseller capabilities depend on the edition purchased. The proposed funnel shows the intended upgrade structure; final entitlements will be confirmed on the offer.
Are the commissions and contest details final?+
The 50% commission rate, listed prices and $2,000 contest reflect the supplied launch plan. Final platform terms, eligibility, refund handling and prize rules must be confirmed before promotion.
Do you have verified EPC or conversion results?+
No verified ArgonMAX AI EPC or conversion figures are published here. The commission calculator is an illustrative calculation and does not predict your results.
Can I edit the promotional materials?+
Yes. Personalize the swipes, select an angle that fits your audience, and add your approved affiliate link. Use confirmed product capabilities and final launch terms in your promotion. Closing emails are drafts to use only after the deadline is announced.
